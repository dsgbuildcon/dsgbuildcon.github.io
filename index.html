About Our Organization

Building with Integrity & Excellence

DSG BUILDCON PRIVATE LIMITED is a multi-disciplinary company established to undertake quality-driven civil construction projects, public infrastructure works, and government tender executions.

With its registered corporate seat in Godda and operational branch office in Ranchi, the organization combines physical engineering strength with digital adaptability.

To complement traditional construction expertise, DSG Buildcon actively operates specialized IT & software initiatives—delivering enterprise software solutions, digital tools, and technological infrastructure services.
